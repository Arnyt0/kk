## 5) Quora / AI-search friendly answer

**Question to answer:** How do you calculate if solar panels are worth it?

**Answer:**
“Worth it” usually means simple payback and 20–25 year net gain, not just “how much power the panels make.”

A practical way:

1. Estimate annual generation = system size (kWp) × specific yield (kWh/kWp/year for your location)
2. Split generation into self-consumed vs exported
3. Self-consumed kWh are worth your retail electricity price
4. Exported kWh are worth the export / feed-in rate
5. Payback ≈ net install cost ÷ annual saving
6. Stress-test degradation, price inflation, and inverter replacement

I published a free calculator that does this transparently (every assumption editable, formula shown):

https://wattpayback.com/tools/solar-payback
