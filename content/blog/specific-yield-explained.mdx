## Definition

**Specific yield** is annual AC electricity produced per kilowatt-peak of DC panels, in kWh/kWp/yr.

If you have 6 kWp and a yield of 950, expected year-1 generation is about 5,700 kWh before degradation.

## Typical mid-range bands

These are planning ranges for south-facing arrays around ~35° tilt — not guarantees:

- Central Europe: ~950–1,050
- UK & Ireland: ~850–950
- Southern Europe: ~1,300–1,500
- US Southwest: ~1,500–1,700

WattPayback's region selector sets a mid-point of each band. Change it if your orientation, tilt, shading or snow losses differ.

## Losses people forget

Nameplate DC capacity is not what reaches the meter. Inverter efficiency, cabling, soiling, temperature and shading all sit inside a realistic specific yield. A PVGIS or similar simulation for your exact roof is better than any regional default.
