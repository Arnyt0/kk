## The rule

Break-even electricity/gas price ratio ≈ **SCOP × boiler seasonal efficiency**.

Example: SCOP 3.5 and boiler efficiency 0.88 → ratio ≈ 3.08. If electricity costs less than about 3.08× the gas unit rate (on a kWh basis), the heat pump wins on energy — before standing charges.

## SCOP is not peak COP

Marketing COP at mild temperatures is not seasonal performance. Use a design SCOP for your climate and emitters.
