## Stack fabric first

Heat you never lose does not need a heat pump or a solar panel. Loft insulation, cavity fill and draught-proofing frequently clear payback hurdles that batteries miss — when the starting fabric is poor.

Apply measures **multiplicatively** in the [insulation payback tool](/tools/insulation-payback): you cannot save the same kilowatt-hour twice.

## Then measure appliances

A plug meter plus the [appliance running cost calculator](/tools/appliance-running-cost) will often find a dryer, old fridge or always-on PC that dwarfs LED bulb savings.
