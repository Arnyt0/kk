## Who benefits

EV drivers, households with timers on tanks and appliances, and people who can pre-heat or pre-cool on a schedule.

## Who loses

Homes with inflexible evening cooking and heating peaks, low overnight use, and no storage. A higher peak rate can erase night savings.
