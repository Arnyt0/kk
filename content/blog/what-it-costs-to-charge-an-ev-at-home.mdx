## From road to wall

Energy from the wall ≈ (km/100) × (kWh/100 km) ÷ charging_efficiency

Charging losses of **10–15%** (efficiency 85–90%) are a normal planning band — not a universal constant.

## Tariffs dominate

A night rate at a third of the day rate usually beats shaving a few percent off vehicle consumption. If you cannot charge off-peak, compare honestly against petrol with the [EV charging calculator](/tools/ev-charging-cost).
