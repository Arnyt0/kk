## Ask these before you sign

1. **What specific yield (kWh/kWp/yr) is assumed**, and for which orientation/tilt/shading?
2. **What self-consumption %** is in the savings table — and from which load profile?
3. **Is the export rate** today's tariff, a forecast, or a promotional figure?
4. **Are savings gross or net of standing charges** and tax?
5. **What is included in the installed price** (scaffolding, electrics, roof work, bird mesh)?
6. **Inverter warranty and expected replacement** — who pays labour at year 12?
7. **Panel performance warranty curve** — what residual output at year 25?
8. **Who owns the system** if financed — and what happens if you move?
9. **Monitoring access** — will you get granular generation and export data?
10. **What happens if yield is 15% lower** than the brochure? Ask for that sensitivity in writing.

Reproduce their headline payback in the [solar payback calculator](/tools/solar-payback) with *your* numbers. If you cannot, ask why their assumptions differ.

This page is general information, not advice. Get an independent survey before spending money.
