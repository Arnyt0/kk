## Two numbers that matter

1. **Standing charge** — currency/day regardless of use
2. **Unit rate** — currency/kWh, sometimes different by time band

Solar and efficiency measures mostly attack the unit-rate portion. Standing charges still matter for heat-pump vs gas comparisons and tariff ranking.

## Map bands before comparing tariffs

If you are on time-of-use pricing, estimate peak / off-peak / night shares from smart-meter data, then use the [tariff comparison](/tools/tariff-comparison) tool.
