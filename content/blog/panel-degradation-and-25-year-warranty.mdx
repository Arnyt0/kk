## What 0.5%/yr means

If year-1 output is 100%, a 0.5%/yr linear model implies roughly 88% by year 25 (compounded slightly differently depending on formula). WattPayback applies a simple compound factor each year.

## Warranty vs. prediction

A performance warranty usually promises that output will not fall below a curve (for example 80–85% at year 25) under defined test conditions. It is a **floor**, not a forecast of your roof.

## Inverters are the other mid-life cost

Many string inverters are replaced once around year 10–15. Include that cash outflow explicitly — the [payback calculator](/tools/solar-payback) does.
